Frequently asked

How much does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages and features you need. After a short chat we'll send a clear quote listing everything that's included.

How do I pay?

You can pay by M-Pesa. For bigger projects we usually split payment into a deposit and a final payment at launch.

Do I need to understand technology?

No. We handle the technical side, explain everything in plain language, and train you and your staff on anything we build.

Can you update my website after it's built?

Yes. Our care plan covers hosting, updates and small changes like prices, photos and opening hours.

Do you work with businesses outside Limuru?

Yes. We're based in Limuru, but we can work with businesses anywhere over WhatsApp, phone and email.
