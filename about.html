About GO-TECH

Technology that works for local businesses

GO-TECH started with a simple observation: many businesses around Limuru and Kiambu are good at what they do, but customers can't find them online, and their owners spend hours on paperwork that a simple system could handle.

We bring those businesses online and automate their daily routines. That means websites and web applications, logos and branding, graphic design and printing, social media management and advertising, all from one local team.

We explain things in plain language, agree on prices before we start, and stay available after launch.
